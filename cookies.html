Cookies Policy

Last Updated: October 28, 2026

1. What Are Cookies and Local Storage?

Cookies are small text files a website can place on your computer or phone. Local storage is a similar place in your own browser where a website can keep information on your device.

2. What Our Website Uses

Our website does not set any cookies of its own. Our online quote tool uses your browser's local storage in two ways:

  • A random ID and session code that let our server match your updates to the same quote request. They contain nothing about you.
  • Your saved answers, only if you tap "Save and finish later" (never license numbers or EINs), so you can pick up where you left off on that device. They are removed when you send your request.

You can clear these anytime by clearing your browser's data for our site.

3. What We Don't Use

We do not use advertising cookies, advertising pixels, social media or ad-network tracking tags, or any website analytics tool. If that ever changes, we will update this policy and our Privacy Policy first.

4. Content From Other Companies

Some pages load content from other companies. Those companies receive your IP address and may set their own cookies under their own privacy policies:

  • A font service: text styles on our pages
  • An icon service: icons on our pages
  • A maps service: the office maps on our homepage
  • A public government vehicle-information service: only if you enter a VIN in our quote tool

Links to our social media page and our Client Center (our policy service portal) take you to other websites only if you click them. Those sites have their own cookie and privacy policies.

5. How to Manage Cookies

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies and clear a site's stored data. Check your browser's help menu for instructions. Blocking content from other companies may change how fonts, icons, or maps appear.

6. Changes to This Policy

We will post any changes here and update the "Last Updated" date above.
